About PrivySpace

PrivySpace is a simple social media website made for a Web Development Midterm.

The website allows users to create an account, login, make simple posts, like posts, and view a profile.

Our Goal

Our goal is to practice building a multi-page website while also reminding users to be careful with the information they share online.
